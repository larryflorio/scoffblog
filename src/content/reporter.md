## F.F. (United States Fantasy Football Court of Appeals)

- *The Commissioner Says So*, 1 F.F. 1 (perpetual): the Commissioner's jurisdiction over the Draft, the League, and his own contentment is plenary and unreviewable. Cited in the Memorandum Opinion and Order, No. 26-FF-0831.
- *Goldich v. Dylus*, 2 F.F. 10 (2026) ("Come on, man."): "I don't want to" begins, rather than ends, an abuse-of-discretion analysis. Cited in the Notice of Appeal.
- *In re League Dues*, 4 F.F. 11 (2026) ("Payment withheld pending further review."): dues obligations to the Commissioner may be unenforceable during the pendency of litigation against him. Cited by the appellant in the League chat, decided by no one; precedent under "too late."
- *Robinson v. Dylus et al.* (2025), unreported: Jacob Robinson's prior-term complaints against the Commissioner. No record survives.
- *Schrodinger v. Dylus*, 3 F.F. 1 (2026): an appeal "remains pending whether or not the Commissioner opens the PDF." Cited in the Emergency Motion to Compel.
