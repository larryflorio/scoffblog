## Background

The League Draft is scheduled for Tuesday, September 8, 2026. Appellant, a league member, moved the Commissioner to move it to 10:00 p.m. so that he and one other member could attend Back-to-School Night at their children's school, an event scheduled by persons Appellant describes as possessing "even greater arbitrary authority than the Commissioner: school administrators."

The Commissioner denied the motion. His opinion identified the governing standard as whether he, "in his sole and gloriously arbitrary discretion, feels like granting the relief sought," and resolved the question in three words.

Appellant appealed to the United States Fantasy Football Court of Appeals, which did not exist until he created it. The Commissioner responded that he was "not reading this essay" and referred the matter to the Office of the Clerk, which is a trash bin. Appellant moved to compel. Appellant then petitioned this Court for certiorari before judgment, on the ground that the court below cannot render judgment because the court below is also the Commissioner, and the Commissioner is in the trash bin's jurisdiction, not the other way around.

## What the Court granted

The petition is granted on all four questions. The case is set for expedited argument because the draft is Tuesday and the Court, unlike the Commissioner, is aware of the calendar.

The Court also directed the parties to brief the antecedent question of whether the "Phantom Clerk Doctrine," under which a referral to a nonexistent officer terminates review, is consistent with *Schrodinger v. Commissioner*, 3 F.F. 1 (2026), which held that an appeal "remains pending whether or not the Commissioner opens the PDF."
